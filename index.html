Choose your weapons

A new pool of 20 weapons every game. Once a weapon is picked, it is gone for both players.

      Two players take turns on one screen. Keys: ← → angle, ↑ ↓ power (hold Shift for big steps), A / D = drive (limited each turn), 1-9 = choose weapon, Space = fire, N = rematch, M = music on/off, Esc = menu. Click anywhere once to switch the sound on.
