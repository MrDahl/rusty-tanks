## The prompts
Use the same STYLE + LAYOUT block for both sheets. Attach one of your existing sheets as a style reference.

```
STYLE + LAYOUT (paste this at the start of both sheet prompts)

Create a sprite sheet of weapon icons for a retro neon artillery game, image size 2400x1792 (4:3).

LAYOUT: exactly 4 columns x 4 rows = 16 cells of identical size, in a strict regular grid with equal gutters (about 60 px) and an equal margin around the grid. Each cell is a rounded rectangle with a 6 px neon outline (outline colour matches the subject: fire = orange, ice = cyan, toxic = green, earth = brown, metal = blue) filled with solid dark navy #0a0f24. The icon is centred in its cell, fills about 75% of the cell width, and never touches the outline. The sheet background behind the cells is flat, solid #05070f.
NO TEXT of any kind: no labels, no names, no numbers, no letters, no captions under the cells. No CRT bezel, no scan lines, no vignette, no border around the whole sheet, no watermark.

ART STYLE: high-detail 16-bit pixel art, premium retro arcade look, neon on dark. Chunky visible pixels with clean hand-placed pixel clusters, 3-5 shades per colour, bold dark outlines on solid objects, strong glow on fire and energy. Same lighting in every icon (light from the upper left, bright rim light). Palette: saturated neon accents (cyan #35f2ff, magenta #ff3df0, amber #ffb347, toxic green #9aff6a, ice blue #9fe8ff); earth in warm browns with a thin bright-green grass line on top; fire in orange and yellow; metal in cool steel grey with cyan highlights.
READABILITY: every icon must be recognisable when shrunk to 64 px wide. ONE main subject per cell, bold silhouette, at most 3-4 key colours, high contrast against the dark navy, no tiny details, no clutter. Ground-shaping icons are drawn as a side-view cross-section of soil (brown layers, thin green grass line on top) with the shape of the hole or heap clearly visible and glowing at its edges.
ORIGINAL DESIGNS ONLY: do not copy the icons of any existing game. Fill all 16 cells; keep all 16 cells in the same style as each other.
```
